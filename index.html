01 / THE PERSONBEYOND THE BULLET POINTS

One brain.
Many tabs open.

I’m Sonakshi Prasad, a Data Science student at NMIMS, Mumbai, with one foot in analytical problem-solving and the other in the creative world.

I enjoy working with data, exploring how systems behave, and turning complex information into something clear. Outside the screen, I’m drawn to painting, art and craft, visual communication, and the energy of bringing an idea to life with a team.

I like being organised, staying open to conversations, and making sure the details get the attention they deserve. For me, creativity and analysis aren’t opposites — they’re two ways of making sense of the world.
